APART

The film is about a wife & husband who don’t understand each other’s way of love. Their private, public and social communication is always muddled with ego, insecurity and guilt. But the love they have for each other, is felt, when they maintain a distance. However, the couple’s togetherness is painful for their two little children. And there is always enough fun even in a dark situation for our notorious little girls.

Until, the elder daughter sees her mother suffer and makes it her business to split her parents.

The film looks at a love marriage gone wrong - where two different cultures, two different backgrounds, two different personalities - try hard to shape up two free kids.

LOVELESS

“Loveless is a series for four interconnected short films combined to feature length duration. These stories are about individuals who take extreme measures to fight their loneliness. Through this film we wish to fuse the theme of of urban loneliness, with other socially relevant issues such as mental health and sexuality.”

GFE

David a young man trying to deal with a recent break up calls for an escort. Things take a funny turn when he gets more than he bargained for. Instead of his physical needs being met, he is bombarded with emotional intimacy that ends up fulfilling a craving he did not even realize he had.
